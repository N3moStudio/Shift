# Make Shift better

Ideas, fixes and independent forks are welcome. You do not need permission or
to notify N3mo Studio before using, changing, showcasing or redistributing the
software. Keep the MIT and applicable third-party notices with copies.

For a bug, include the Shift/iOS versions, installation method, reproduction
steps and expected result. Share only reviewed, redacted logs; pairing files,
Apple credentials, helper keys and precise private locations do not belong in
public issues. Feature requests work best when they explain the problem first.

For a pull request, describe the change and what you tested. Follow
[the build guide](docs/BUILDING.md), run checks appropriate to the change, and
identify anything still needing an iPhone. If you change helper files, rebuild
their bundled ZIPs. Preserve compatibility or clearly document migrations.

Contributions are offered under the repository’s MIT license unless explicitly
identified as a separately licensed third-party component. Retain its notices.
Only submit material you have the right to contribute. No copyright assignment
or separate contributor agreement is required.

If you distribute a modified fork, clearly identify your version and its author
so people know who maintains it. Do not imply it is an official N3mo Studio,
Apple, Locus or idevice release. This does not add a restriction to the MIT
copyright permissions, and no registered-trademark claim is made here.
